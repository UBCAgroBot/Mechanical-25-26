Under Chassis/Powertrain/Steering - Have your subassemblies saved here including all parts and a master subassembly of the subsystem

Instructions on likely best way to complete this:

	To integrate, please pack and go each subsystem into the subassembly folder if you have the subassembly

	If you do not have a subassembly and only integrated into a main CAD, you can create a new subassembly with the following steps:
		1. Ctr + Select the parts that you want in a subassembly
		2. Right-click
		3. Choose form new assembly
		4. fix any broken mates. (sometimes faster to just delete the mates and remake them)
